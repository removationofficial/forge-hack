# Tomato early blight (sample knowledge)

Early blight shows as brown concentric-ring spots on older leaves, which then yellow and drop. It spreads in warm, humid weather.

Remove and destroy infected lower leaves. Avoid overhead watering; water at the base in the morning. Spray neem oil at 3 ml per litre of water every 7 days, or copper oxychloride at 3 g per litre as directed on the label.

Rotate with non-solanaceous crops for at least 2 seasons.

# PM-KISAN (sample knowledge -- verify before you ship real content)

PM-KISAN provides income support of Rs 6,000 per year to eligible farmer families, paid in three instalments. Eligibility generally requires cultivable land ownership and a bank account linked with Aadhaar. Institutional landholders and many high-income categories are excluded. Apply through the PM-KISAN portal or the nearest Common Service Centre.

# Kisan Credit Card (KCC)

KCC gives farmers short-term credit for cultivation at concessional interest. Apply at a bank branch or cooperative bank with land records and identity proof.
